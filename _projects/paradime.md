For the Inter-IIT Tech Meet product challenge, our team proposed two connected
ways to strengthen Paradime's community marketplace. **Dimes** were a virtual
currency for rewarding useful contributions; **slogs** placed code snippets
beside data documentation so knowledge could be reused without leaving the
workflow.

The proposal covered user journeys, incentives, marketplace behaviour, and a
path from product requirements to implementation. It won a gold medal at
Inter-IIT Tech Meet 11.0, and the code-and-documentation idea was later
reflected in Paradime Docs.
