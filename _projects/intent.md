I built Intent for my own needs: a home screen made of rows of widgets that
scroll both ways, with money tracking from bank texts, time tracking, and a
family tree built from contacts. Everything stays on the phone. I have made it
public in case someone else faces similar problems.

[See what Intent does](https://p-prakhar.github.io/intent-launcher/).
