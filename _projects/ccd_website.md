As Overall Placement Coordinator, I helped maintain the official Centre for
Career Development website alongside the placement and internship portals and
their Linux server. The public site presented recruiter-facing information,
student resources, schedules, and contact paths for IIT Guwahati's placement
process.

The work combined front-end maintenance in Bootstrap with the operational side
of keeping the portal and server available during a time-sensitive placement
season.
