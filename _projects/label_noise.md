This research project introduced controlled amounts of incorrect labels into a
paraphrase-detection dataset and measured how model quality changed as the
noise increased. The goal was to distinguish headline accuracy from actual
robustness when training data is imperfect.

The repository contains the experiment pipeline, noise-generation steps, and
comparative evaluation used to study that sensitivity.

[Browse the source](https://github.com/p-prakhar/label-noise-analysis).
