Questions

Before you switch.

Does it read my SMS on a server?

No. Messages are read on the phone and stay there. Intent has no server to send them to.

Will it slow my phone down?

No. Search and launch are instant, nothing polls in the background, and widgets refresh only while you can see them. It runs well on 4 GB budget phones.

Can I use only Money, or only Focus?

Yes. Turn on just the parts you want. Everything keeps working, and the combined views appear when both are on.

What happens after the free week?

The home screen stays free. Money, Focus and Family need Pro, but nothing is deleted and your data keeps recording while you decide.

Can I move to a new phone?

Yes. Make one encrypted backup, locked with your own password, and restore it on the new phone.

Which phones does it support?

Android 12 and newer. Built and tested on Pixel, Samsung, OnePlus and vivo, from flagships to budget phones.
