Intent Launcher privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Intent Launcher is a home screen app for Android made by Prakhar ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your data stays on your phone, and I never receive it unless you write to me yourself.

The person responsible for the app and this policy (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and the "controller" under the EU and UK GDPR) is Prakhar, an individual developer in India, reachable at prakhar.workmail@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into the app, and everything it reads to work, stays on your phone and is never sent to me or anyone else:

  • your home screen layout, widgets and settings;
  • contacts and calendar events, which are read live from your phone to show and search them;
  • bank and UPI SMS, which Money reads on the phone to record transactions;
  • if you turn it on, payment notifications from UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and similar), read on the phone to record payments your bank sent no SMS for;
  • money, budgets and investments you enter;
  • family trees, relationships, birthdays and photos;
  • time tracking, habits, to-dos, routines and app usage;
  • if you connect Health Connect, your steps, exercise sessions and sleep sessions, read only to tick habits such as "Evening walk", "Gym" or "In bed by 11" (nothing is written to Health Connect, and health data is never used for ads or shared);
  • if you allow physical activity, whether the phone is walking or in a vehicle, used to suggest tracking a commute and to tick walks;
  • names of chats taken from notifications so search can find them (message text is never read or kept);
  • the amount of any donation you record.

I have no server that stores user data, and the app has no account, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.

When you write to me

If you contact me on WhatsApp or by email (Settings > Help & support), I receive what you send: your message, your number or address, the app version and phone model, and the last crash report if you leave that box ticked. I use it only to reply and fix bugs, keep it no longer than needed for that (at most a year), and delete it sooner if you ask.

When you share something yourself

Some actions open another app with text filled in, and nothing is sent unless you send it there: contacting me on WhatsApp or by email, sharing a bank SMS Money couldn't read (long numbers are hidden first), sharing a family tree file (locked with a password you choose), searching the web in your browser, and paying through your UPI app. What those apps do with it is covered by their own policies.

Backups

Backups are encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256) and saved only to the folder you pick. If that folder syncs to a cloud service, that service's policy applies to the file. I can't open your backups.

Permissions

The app asks for permissions such as contacts, calendar, SMS, location, notifications and usage access only for the features that need them. Every permission and what it's used for is listed in the app under Settings > Privacy & permissions, where you can also turn each one off.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export (Settings > Backup & restore) and delete (Settings > Clear data, or uninstall) everything the app keeps, at any time, on the phone itself. Since I hold none of it, I can't see or sell it. For anything you sent me, write to me to access, correct or delete it, or to nominate someone to act for you. Under India's DPDP Act you may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India; in the EU or UK, to your data protection authority. I don't sell or share personal information as defined by California law, and nothing is used for ads or profiling.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is performing what you asked the app to do (contract) for the services listed above, and my legitimate interest in replying to messages you send me. The weather, map and Google services may process requests outside your country (Open-Meteo in the EU, OpenStreetMap in the EU and UK, Google worldwide).

Children

Intent Launcher isn't meant for children under 18 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

Deleting your data

Since nothing is kept off your phone, uninstalling the app or using Settings > Clear data removes all of it. Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

Changes and contact
